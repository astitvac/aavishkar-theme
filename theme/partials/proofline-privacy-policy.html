Summary

At a glance: Proofline is a knowledge management tool designed to help teams capture and organize decisions. We collect only what's necessary to provide our service, keep your data secure and private, and never sell your information to third parties.

  • What we collect: Email, workspace data you create in Proofline, basic usage analytics
  • Why we collect it: To provide and improve the Proofline service
  • Who sees it: Only you, your team, and Aavishkar LLC employees (when needed for support)
  • Where it's stored: Secure cloud infrastructure with encryption
  • Your control: You can export, delete, or restrict access to your data at any time

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Proofline, a product developed by Aavishkar LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). It describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use:

  • The Proofline web application (including beta versions)
  • Any Proofline APIs or integrations
  • Associated services and support channels

This policy does NOT cover:

  • The main Aavishkar.ai website (covered by a separate policy)
  • Third-party services you connect to Proofline (e.g., Google Workspace, GitHub)
  • Your organization's internal policies regarding data handling

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: Email address, name, organization name
  • Workspace content: Documents, decisions, hypotheses, insights, comments, and other data you create in Proofline
  • Team data: Information about team members you invite to your workspace
  • Support communications: Messages you send to our support team
  • Billing information: Payment details (processed securely through Stripe; we do not store full credit card numbers)

Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage data: Features you use, pages you visit, actions you take within Proofline
  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, device identifiers
  • Log data: Error reports, performance metrics, access times
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Session tokens, preferences, analytics cookies

Information from Third-Party Integrations

If you connect Proofline to third-party services (e.g., Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub), we may receive:

  • Profile information (name, email, avatar)
  • Access tokens to enable integrations
  • Metadata about connected resources (file names, commit messages, etc.)

Important: We only request the minimum permissions necessary for each integration to function. You can revoke these permissions at any time.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

Provide and Maintain the Service

  • Store and sync your workspace data across devices
  • Enable collaboration features (sharing, comments, permissions)
  • Process payments and maintain subscriptions
  • Send transactional emails (password resets, team invitations, billing notices)

Improve and Develop Proofline

  • Analyze usage patterns to identify popular features and pain points
  • Debug errors and optimize performance
  • Conduct research to develop new features
  • Test and refine user experience

Communicate with You

  • Respond to support requests and questions
  • Send important service updates and security notices
  • Provide onboarding guidance and product tips (you can opt out)
  • Request feedback on your experience

Ensure Security and Compliance

  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect the rights and safety of our users

How We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell your personal information or workspace data to third parties. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

Within Your Workspace

  • Your workspace content is visible to team members you've invited
  • Access is controlled by permissions you set (workspace admin, editor, viewer)
  • Team members can see basic profile information of other members

Service Providers

We work with trusted vendors who help us operate Proofline:

  • Cloud hosting: Amazon Web Services (AWS) or Google Cloud Platform (data storage and computing)
  • Payment processing: Stripe (billing and subscriptions)
  • Email delivery: SendGrid or similar (transactional emails)
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar (usage insights)
  • Support tools: Intercom, Zendesk, or similar (customer support)

These providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for services we've authorized.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to:

  • Comply with valid legal process (subpoena, court order, etc.)
  • Enforce our Terms of Service or other agreements
  • Protect the security or integrity of our service
  • Prevent harm to our users or the public

When legally permitted, we will notify you before disclosing your information.

Business Transfers

If Aavishkar LLC is acquired, merged, or sells assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity. We will notify you of any such change and your options regarding your data.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specific retention periods:

  • Workspace content: Retained until you delete it or close your account
  • Account information: Retained until you request account deletion
  • Usage logs: Typically retained for 90 days (longer if needed for security investigations)
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes
  • Support communications: Retained for 3 years or until resolved

After deletion, we may retain certain data in backups for up to 90 days, after which it is permanently deleted.

Important: Deleted data cannot be recovered. We recommend exporting important information before deletion.

Security Measures

We take data security seriously and implement industry-standard protections:

Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access controls: Multi-factor authentication, role-based permissions, least-privilege access
  • Infrastructure security: Regular security patches, firewall protection, intrusion detection
  • Secure development: Code reviews, vulnerability scanning, penetration testing

Organizational Safeguards

  • Background checks for employees with data access
  • Regular security training for staff
  • Incident response plan and security monitoring
  • Third-party security audits (planned for 2026)

Your Responsibility

  • Use strong, unique passwords for your Proofline account
  • Enable multi-factor authentication when available
  • Do not share your account credentials
  • Report suspicious activity to ac@aavishkar.ai

No system is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to us immediately.

International Data Transfers

Proofline is operated from the United States. If you access Proofline from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your home country. By using Proofline, you consent to this transfer and processing.

European Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases for processing:

  • Contract performance: Processing necessary to provide Proofline services
  • Legitimate interests: Improving our product, fraud prevention, security
  • Consent: Optional features like marketing emails (you can withdraw anytime)
  • Legal obligations: Compliance with laws and regulations

For data transfers outside the EEA, we use Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

California Users (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Right to request deletion of personal information
  • Right to opt out of "sales" of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at ac@aavishkar.ai.

Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your data. Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

Access and Portability

  • View your data: Access all workspace content via the Proofline application
  • Export your data: Download your workspace in JSON format (Settings → Export Data)
  • Request a copy: Contact ac@aavishkar.ai for a comprehensive data export

Correction and Deletion

  • Update information: Edit your profile, workspace content, and settings anytime
  • Delete content: Remove documents, comments, or other workspace data
  • Close your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account (permanent and irreversible)

Marketing and Communications

  • Opt out of marketing emails: Click "Unsubscribe" in any promotional email
  • Control notifications: Adjust email preferences in Settings → Notifications
  • Note: You cannot opt out of transactional emails (password resets, billing notices) while using Proofline

Cookies and Tracking

  • Essential cookies: Required for Proofline to function (cannot be disabled)
  • Analytics cookies: Can be disabled in Settings → Privacy or via browser settings
  • Third-party cookies: Managed by your browser's privacy settings

Object to Processing

If you are in the EEA, you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact ac@aavishkar.ai to exercise this right.

Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you may file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Google API Services

Proofline integrates with Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Docs) to enhance your knowledge capture workflow. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What We Access

  • Google Drive: Read access to files you explicitly connect to Proofline
  • Gmail: Read access to emails you reference in decisions or hypotheses
  • Google Docs: Read/write access to documents you link to Proofline
  • Profile information: Name, email, avatar (for authentication and display)

How We Use Google Data

  • Display within Proofline: Show previews, titles, and metadata of connected files
  • Enable collaboration: Link decisions to relevant documents and emails
  • Sync changes: Update content when you modify connected Google files

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do NOT transfer Google user data to third parties (except as required to provide Proofline)
  • We do NOT use Google data for advertising or marketing
  • We do NOT sell or rent Google data
  • We do NOT allow humans to read your Google data except for limited purposes (security, compliance, bug fixes) and only with your consent

Revoking Access

You can revoke Proofline's access to your Google account at any time:

  • In Proofline: Settings → Integrations → Disconnect Google
  • In Google: Google Account Permissions → Remove Proofline

Revoking access will disable Google-related features but will not delete your Proofline workspace.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors.

How We Notify You

  • Minor changes: Updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Significant changes: Email notification to all active users at least 30 days before changes take effect
  • Material changes: Prominent notice in the Proofline application requiring acknowledgment

Your Options

If you disagree with updated terms, you may:

  • Export your data before the changes take effect
  • Close your account (Settings → Account → Delete Account)
  • Contact us to discuss concerns at ac@aavishkar.ai

Continued use of Proofline after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
