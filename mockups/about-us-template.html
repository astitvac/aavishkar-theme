What is Aavishkar

Accelerating Scientific Discovery with AI

At the intersection of artificial intelligence and scientific inquiry lies a profound opportunity ‑ not to replace human insight, but to amplify our collective ability to understand the universe.

Aavishkar, meaning "discovery/invention" in Sanskrit, represents our contribution to this emerging paradigm of human‑AI collaboration in science. We build AI-powered expert systems that put powerful research tools in every scientist's hands.

Our mission is to create intelligent systems that assist scientists in literature review, data analysis, experimental design, and hypothesis generation - enabling faster breakthroughs and deeper insights across scientific disciplines.
