Key Concepts

Knowledge Card

A single insight, finding, or note—captured in a structured format. Draft freely, then save to make it permanent and part of your timeline.

Proofline

Your visual research timeline. See how ideas connect and build on each other. Every card links to its sources—creating a traceable history of your thinking.

AI-Powered Search

Ask questions about your documents in plain English. AI finds relevant sections and cites its sources—so you can verify every answer.

Citations & Sources

Every Knowledge Card links to its source—whether that's a document you uploaded or another card. See where any idea came from with one click.
